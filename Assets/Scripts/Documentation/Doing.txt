Current Goal: Write Interaction System

		( Using Lobby System )

		Ownership Interface & Assigment System

		> Owner Interface: Defines Who / What is the current Owner
		> Owner Controller: Allow All Valid Ownership Interaction
		  
		
		Relationship System
		
		> Relationship has Two Ints of Ranges 0 to 1000 Influence Points 

		> Default InPeace which lets you develop your relationship with 
		other players and lets you access certain perks at certain tiers
		
		> InPeace has no Cap on how well you develop your relationship 
		but only 1000 is decay protected anything above needs to retained
		by acting in favour of the said owner.

		> If InPeace reaches sub 100, the other Owner has a arsenal of dmg
		to send you way. So try to stay above it, but if a war does breaks 
		out, then your InPeace Range will be reduced to 0 and swapped with 
		the InWar Range which will decay further down in contrast combat

		
		> Relationship Tiers Exist for both 100 - 250 - 500 - 750 - 1000
		



		Define Relationship System

		> Define Relations between Owners 

			> Each relation has a development parameter to show
			players what their potential relations can be, also
			serves as a social status progress bar with all the
			owners in your game session. At the beginning start
			with the default values, and as the game progresses
			and the choices players do reflect onto their other
			owners relations. 
			
			Conflicting playstyles will drain points and reduce
			gains, potential chances from certain owners can be
			greatly reduced. 

			While relationships which share mutually beneficial 
			playstyles will grant players influence points. And 
			these influence points lets the player access deals
			with the other owners that are unique, it will also
			grant the player certain oppertunities other miss. 
			
			Finding the balance between relations is the key to 
			everything from gaining favours, weapons, to intel. 
			
			Retaining relations thru Diplomacy get more complex 
			as the game grows on. Fragile relationships will be
			exploited by opposite forces. The Game is all about
			Letting the late game Power fantasy loose and other
			owners can create extremely strong ties to rely on.

			Their allegiance will show in your relation, so be
			careful because that can come around real quick if
			handled poorly.
			

			> The Parameters range is 1000p long, and each step
			of the way defines a new standing depending on who,
			your talking to.
