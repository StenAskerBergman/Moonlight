1. Island Inventory

Purpose:
To serve as the central data structure for all items that are 
present on the island. It doesn't handle the logic or interactions 
of these items; it merely stores and provides access to them.

Proposed Name: IslandInventory

Responsibilities:

Store a list or dictionary of items and their quantities.
Provide methods to check the quantity of an item.
Provide methods to add or remove items (quantity adjustments).

------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Island Inventory Manager

Purpose:
This script is responsible for high-level management of the entire 
inventory system for the island. It would interact with the Island 
Inventory, decide when items move, get produced, get consumed, etc. 
It's the brain of the inventory system for the island.

Proposed Name: IslandInventorySystem

Responsibilities:

Interact with IslandInventory to get, set, or adjust items.
Coordinate with other systems (like production, consumption, 
transportation).

Handle events or triggers related to the inventory (e.g., when an 
item is produced or consumed).

Act as a central point to communicate with other inventory 
managers (like BuildingInventoryManager or BoatInventoryManager).


------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Island Item Manager

Purpose:
This script would handle the specific behaviors & characteristics 
of individual item types on the island. For example, the growth 
pattern of wheat, the decay of perishable items, or the specific 
use of a particular tool.


Proposed Name: IslandItemBehaviorManager

Responsibilities:

Define and manage specific behaviors of individual items (e.g., 
decay, growth). Handle any item-specific events or triggers.
Coordinate with IslandInventorySystem to reflect any changes or 
behaviors in the central inventory.

------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Island Storage

Purpose:
A representation of the physical storage facilities on the island. 
It dictates how much of each item or total items the island can 
store. It might also be used to influence game mechanics, like if 
storage is full, certain processes might stop.

Proposed Name: IslandStorageCapacity

Responsibilities:

Define the maximum capacity or individual capacities of different 
item types. Provide methods to check if the storage is full or if 
there's enough space for a certain item. Potentially trigger events 
when storage is full or nearing full capacity.
