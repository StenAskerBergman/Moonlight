-- Answer --

Because ItemSlot is being created before its ItemStack exists.

So ItemSlot.Awake() expects an ItemStack child immediately.

The runtime sequence from your stack trace is:

----

UnitInventory.Awake()
    ↓
InitializeItemSlots()
    ↓
CreateNewItemSlot()
    ↓
newGameObject.AddComponent<ItemSlot>()
    ↓
ItemSlot.Awake() FIRES IMMEDIATELY

----




Your architecture intentionally expects:
-
ItemSlot GameObject
└── ItemStack GameObject
-
ItemSlot assumption:
"I have an ItemStack child"

Reality:
"I don't yet"
